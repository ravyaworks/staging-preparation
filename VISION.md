# Vision

To become the most flexible, scalable, and intelligent open platform for building AI-powered conversational experiences — enabling any business to create, deploy, and optimize conversations across every channel their customers use.

## Core Principles

- **Multi-tenant by design** — Every customer is isolated, every resource is partitioned
- **Channel-agnostic** — Build once, deploy everywhere
- **AI-first** — Intelligence is not an add-on; it is the foundation
- **Extensible** — Everything is a plugin, every component can be replaced
- **Developer-friendly** — Great APIs, SDKs, documentation, and tooling
- **Production-grade** — Scalable, secure, observable, and reliable
