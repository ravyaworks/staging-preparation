# Workflows

Pre-built workflow definitions and templates for the Conversation Platform.

## Workflows

- `website-sales/` — Website sales and lead capture
- `support/` — Customer support ticketing
- `onboarding/` — User onboarding flows
- `lead-generation/` — Lead qualification and nurturing
- `appointment/` — Appointment scheduling
- `invoice/` — Invoice and payment flows
- `custom/` — Custom workflow templates
